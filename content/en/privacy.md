## 1. Our Philosophy (Less is Better)

At Estran Studio Inc., we believe that privacy is a fundamental right. Our business model does not rely on selling your data. In fact, the less we have, the better off we are.

In accordance with Quebec's Law 25 (Private Sector Privacy Act), here is what you need to know.

## 2. Automatically Collected Data

When you visit estran.studio, we only collect technical data strictly necessary for the operation and security of the site.

Server Logs

Like any web server, our infrastructure automatically records certain information when you connect:
- Your IP address (anonymized after a short period).
- Browser type and operating system.
- The page visited and the time of the request.

Why? This data is used solely to:
- Detect and block cyberattacks (DDoS, bots).
- Diagnose technical errors (bugs).

It is never cross-referenced with your real identity.

Cookies

We do not use any third-party advertising cookies.
The site may use local storage (localStorage) to remember your theme preference (Dark/Light). That's it.

## 3. Data You Provide to Us

We only collect personal information if you choose to send it to us voluntarily, for example:
- By email: If you write to us, we keep your email address to respond to you.
- On GitHub/Discord: Your interactions on these platforms are governed by their respective privacy policies. We do not import this data onto our servers.

## 4. Data Sharing

We never sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties.
