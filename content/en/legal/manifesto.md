This manifesto defines the values that guide every project at Estran Studio.

## I. Sovereignty

We believe users should be the sole masters of their tools and data. We reject models that create lock-in and technological black boxes. Building with Estran means choosing autonomy.

## II. Performance by Design

Performance is not an option; it's a mark of respect for the user and the hardware. We do not build bloated software. Every line of code must justify its existence through utility and efficiency.

## III. Digital Craftsmanship

We treat software as a piece of precision craftsmanship. We prioritize robustness and clarity over unnecessary complexity. We build for longevity.

## IV. Resilience

Inspired by the "estran" — the zone between land and sea in constant motion — we cultivate an adaptable and resilient approach. We operate with agility in demanding technical environments.

## V. Technical Ethics

Technology should augment human capabilities, not alienate them. Our solutions are designed to empower the individual while protecting their digital dignity.
