Harmonium is our exploration platform for algorithmic music. It's not just an instrument; it's a training engine for exploring complex harmonic structures through code.

### Vision
- **Procedural Generation**: Creating evolving sound textures.
- **Assisted Training**: Tools for musicians looking to push their theoretical limits.
- **Modular Architecture**: Designed for integration into other creative environments.

*Note: Harmonium is currently in active R&D phase.*
